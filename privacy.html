Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This page explains what happens to your data when you use aryanmods (the “site”). It is written to be read, not to hide behind. The short version: the site has no accounts, no tracking of its own, and stores two small things in your browser.

1. What we store on your device

Two values in localStorage, both of which stay in your browser:

  • Theme — whether you chose dark or light, so it does not reset on every page.
  • Ad choice — “granted” or “denied”, plus the date you answered.

Neither is sent to us, because there is nothing server-side to send it to. Clearing site data in your browser removes both.

2. Ads

The site is free and may be supported by advertising. Ads are served by Google AdSense and are off by default — no ad script loads, and no advertising cookie is set, until you press “Accept ads” in the banner.

If you accept, Google and its partners may:

  • set and read cookies on this site,
  • use your approximate location, device type and browsing behaviour to choose ads,
  • measure how ads perform.

You can change your mind at any time with in the footer. Declining means no ads and no ad cookies; the rest of the site works exactly the same.

Useful opt-outs, independent of this site:

  • Google ad settings
  • aboutads.info (US)
  • youronlinechoices.com (EU)

3. Affiliate links

Some links in the “Support the site” section are affiliate links. If you buy through them, the shop may pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you. Those shops set their own cookies once you leave this site; their privacy policies apply there. Affiliate links are marked with a badge and carry rel="sponsored".

4. The request form

The app-request form asks for an app name, an optional link, your email and an optional message. It is used only to read your request and reply to it. It is not added to a mailing list, not sold, and not shared. If the form is not connected to a service, nothing is transmitted at all and the site tells you so.

5. Hosting and server logs

The site is static files hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the technical requests your browser makes (IP address, page, browser version, time). That is outside our control and governed by GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not run analytics, pixels or fingerprinting.

6. Downloads and third-party sites

Download links point to files hosted elsewhere. Once you follow one, you are on someone else’s site and their privacy policy applies. We do not receive, store or track what you download.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about visitors, there is normally nothing to export or delete. Your controls are in your own browser:

  • withdraw ad consent — footer → Cookie settings → No thanks
  • erase everything — clear cookies and site data for this domain

If you sent a request through the form and want it deleted, contact us with the same email address and we will remove it. This covers the rights in the GDPR (EU/UK) and India’s DPDP Act; where they apply, you also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child submitted a request, contact us and it will be deleted.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above changes with it. Material changes — a new ad network, for example — will be listed on the What’s new page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the request form on the home page.
